# Content Design

Content is part of the visual design.

## Headlines

Headlines should be:
- specific
- concise
- memorable
- meaningful

Avoid generic phrases such as:
- "The future of..."
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Revolutionize your workflow"
- "Transform your business"
- "Built for the modern world"

unless genuinely appropriate.

## Section copy

Do not fill empty space with unnecessary copy.

Prefer:
- short paragraphs
- strong statements
- useful supporting details

## Product claims

Never invent:
- statistics
- customer names
- testimonials
- awards
- performance claims
- company logos

If information is unavailable, ask or use clearly marked placeholder
content during development.

## Visual relationship

Copy should be designed together with layout.

Consider:
- line length
- headline wrapping
- paragraph width
- whitespace
- hierarchy
